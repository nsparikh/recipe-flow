Classic Minestrone Soup
Serves 6 to 8

INGREDIENTS

4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
1 medium yellow onion, chopped
2 medium carrots, peeled and chopped
2 ribs celery, chopped
1/4 cup tomato paste
2 cups chopped seasonal vegetables (zucchini, yellow squash, green beans or peas)
4 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
1 can (28 ounces) diced tomatoes, with their juices
4 cups vegetable broth
2 cups water
1 teaspoon fine sea salt, plus more to taste
2 bay leaves
Pinch of red pepper flakes
Freshly ground black pepper
1 cup small pasta
1 can (15 ounces) white beans, rinsed and drained
2 cups chopped greens (spinach, kale or chard)
2 teaspoons lemon juice
Freshly grated Parmesan cheese, for garnish (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Warm 3 tablespoons of the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering.

2. Add the onion, carrot, celery, tomato paste and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring often, until the
   onions are translucent, about 8 minutes.

3. Add the seasonal vegetables, garlic, oregano and thyme. Cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.

4. Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juices, the broth, the water, the salt, the bay leaves, the
   red pepper flakes and several grinds of black pepper.

5. Raise the heat and bring to a boil, then partially cover the pot and reduce the heat to a gentle
   simmer.

6. Simmer for 15 minutes.

7. Remove the lid and stir in the pasta, beans and greens.

8. Simmer uncovered until the pasta is al dente, about 20 minutes.

9. Off the heat, remove and discard the bay leaves. Stir in the lemon juice and the remaining
   1 tablespoon olive oil. Season with additional salt and pepper to taste.

10. Garnish bowls with grated Parmesan before serving.
