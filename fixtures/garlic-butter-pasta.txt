Spaghetti with Garlic Butter Sauce
Serves 2

INGREDIENTS

200 g spaghetti
1 teaspoon fine sea salt

For the sauce:
4 tablespoons unsalted butter
3 cloves garlic, minced
2 tablespoons flat-leaf parsley, chopped

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente, about 10 minutes. Drain.

2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a small pan over low heat and add the garlic. Cook gently until
   fragrant, about 3 minutes.

3. Stir the parsley into the sauce.

4. Toss the drained pasta with the sauce and serve.
